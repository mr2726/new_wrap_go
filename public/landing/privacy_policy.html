How We Use Your Data

WrapGO uses the collected data to enable reliable and convenient advertising campaign tracking. We use the data we collect:

  • To verify driver eligibility and maintain the safety, security, and integrity of our services.
  • To calculate mileage for ad campaign payouts and provide precise ROI metrics to our advertisers.
  • To perform internal operations necessary to provide our services, including troubleshooting software bugs.
